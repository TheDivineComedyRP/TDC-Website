About the realm

The Divine Comedy is a roleplay server for Skyrim Special Edition, running on SkyMP (Skyrim multiplayer). The province is emptied of its usual inhabitants — the souls you meet in the world are other players, not the game’s own characters.

Following Dante, the realm is divided into three estates. Where a character lives, and what they may become, is earned through play rather than given at the start.

How to join

Joining will be handled by the launcher: it installs the required mods and connects you to the realm in one step. It is still in development — the button above will offer the download once it is ready.

Built on SkyMP, an open-source Skyrim multiplayer project.
